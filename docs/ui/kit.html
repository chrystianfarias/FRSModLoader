nfsu2.css

Underground 2's own furniture as classes: the glowing green panel, the grey list with its washed selection, the pill buttons, the Pause tiles. Everything is scoped under .nfs, and the whole kit scales from one variable, --nfs-size.

Inside the game the buttons, rings, highlights and icons are drawn with the game's own textures, served from its packs at http://nfsu2.tex/. Opened straight from disk, this page shows the flat version underneath, and the icons are not there.
